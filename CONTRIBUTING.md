# Contributing

Results from your own hardware are welcome: a whole box in `devices/`, or a part in `parts.yaml`.

- **Licence.** Contributions are made under the Apache License 2.0 (the LICENSE file), the same licence the rest of
  this repository carries. By contributing you license your work under it.
- **Sign your commits.** Every commit needs a Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off, which says you wrote the
  change or otherwise have the right to submit it under the licence (DCO 1.1, developercertificate.org):

      Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>

  `git commit -s` adds the line for you. Pull requests with unsigned commits are asked to re-sign, nothing more.
- **Say what you tested.** Every result names the OPNsense version, the Pazuzu version, the date and what happened.
  A failure is as useful as a clean run: say which stage failed and what the page said.
- **Keep private details out.** No serial numbers, IP or hardware addresses, Wi-Fi names, keys or QR codes, in the
  text or in photos.
- **Tiers.** A pull request can add a Community tested or a Known not to work entry. Only Hanbi sets Hanbi Certified.
- **Links.** Leave buy links out, or mark them unpaid. A link never decides a tier.
